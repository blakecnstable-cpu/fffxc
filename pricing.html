Common questions
Is the AI assistant real legal advice? ↓
No. The AI provides general legal information based on UK law — like our free guides, but personalised to your situation. It is not advice from a qualified solicitor. For complex or high-value matters, always consult a solicitor via solicitors.lawsociety.org.uk.
Can I cancel anytime? ↓
Yes. Cancel through your PayPal account at any time. No contracts, no cancellation fees. Your access continues until the end of your current billing period.
What areas of law does the AI cover? ↓
Currently: UK consumer rights (Consumer Rights Act 2015, refunds, faulty goods, online shopping, scams) and online/social media rights (UK GDPR, Online Safety Act 2023, content removal, harassment, defamation). Employment and housing rights are coming soon.
What payment methods do you accept? ↓
We use PayPal, which accepts all major debit and credit cards, bank accounts, and PayPal balances. We never see your card details — PayPal handles everything securely.
Is there a free trial? ↓
The free guides and tools (letter builder, rights checker, SAR generator) don't need an account. For a trial of the AI assistant, email hello@knowyourrights.co.uk and we'll set one up.
